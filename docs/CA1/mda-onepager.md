## One-line pitch
For fantasy tabletop RPG lovers, a strategic and adventerous game where the player fights animals, monsters, and bandits, using limited energy per turn.

## Aesthetics (what the player feels)
- strategy
- tension and relief
- progression

## Core mechanics (3 to 5 verbs or systems)
1. Move
2. Attack
3. Manage energy
4. Heal
5. Take turns

## Dynamics (what emerges when the mechanics meet the player)
- the player can team up against stronger enemies to reward postitioning
- the player can utilise their energy to move, attack, and heal in one turn to reward energy management
