## Short Description
Turn-based fantasy tactics: manage your energy, fight monsters, win each run.

## Long Description
Adventurer's Path is a turn-based fantasy tactics game for tabletop RPG fans. Lead your adventurer into battles against animals, monsters and bandits, where every point of energy matters and every turn is a decision.

Each turn you have a limited pool of energy. Spend it to move, attack or heal. You can do all three in a single turn, but only if you plan it well. Position yourself smartly, pick your moment to strike, and know when to hold back.

FEATURES
- Tactical turn-based combat: move, attack and heal, all paid for with energy
- Energy management: a limited pool each turn forces real choices
- Team up with allies: fight alongside two allies and use positioning to take down stronger enemies
- Three levels to fight through, each with its own challenge
- Three enemy types to learn and outsmart
- Short runs of 5 to 10 minutes, ideal for a quick session
- Built for portrait play with simple tap controls
- Haptic feedback on attacks so every hit feels heavy
- No ads or in-app purchases
